Act 3 · the reveal

The hidden alarm: an extra stop is a symptom, not a weapon

Teammates drive the same car. With an extra stop, the slower qualifier beats the teammate only of the time, against without one (z = ). Qualifying far behind does not make teams gamble on stops (r = ): the extra stop signals trouble.

Season, constructor and circuit filters apply. A driver filter keeps pairs where that driver is either teammate. Bars = 95% confidence range (Wilson).

The call you would make: when a car suddenly needs a stop the plan did not have, treat it as an alarm and protect the other car's result.
